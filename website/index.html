Features

Three ideas, one app.

Everything below runs on your phone. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Privacy

Blocking that never leaves your device.

Spam and phishing detection run entirely on-device, in two clear tiers: built-in, or delegated to an external blocker you already trust. The optional phishing list downloads once; every match after that stays local. Borderline senders get a discreet "suspected spam" label instead of a silent decision made for you.

Theming

Themeable from top to bottom.

A tabbed theme picker with live in-app updates crossfades theme, dark mode and text size, no restart. Pure-black mode for OLED screens, colors you can set per conversation or tie to the app icon, and a launcher icon that swaps instantly and stays in sync with the splash screen.

Aa
Backup

Full local backup, no cloud required.

Back up messages, settings, blocking rules, scheduled messages and the archived or pinned state of your conversations, to a folder or a single .zip, entirely on-device. Automatic backups run on your own schedule, and a built-in manager lets you rename or delete them without leaving the app.

About

Why build another fork?

OpenMessages started from a handful of small improvements I wanted while using Open Messages as my daily SMS app. I use AI tools as part of my workflow, not to blindly generate code, but to understand codebases faster, test ideas, and work more efficiently while still reviewing everything myself.

My first instinct was to contribute upstream. But after a small contribution got judged more on how it was written than on the quality of the result, I decided to maintain my own fork instead: one judged on the final product, not on whether modern tools were involved.

And more

A long list of small things done right.

  • Saved message templates
  • Expandable full-screen message input
  • Redesigned attachment picker
  • Read / unread swipe actions
  • Configurable conversation header
  • Sound on send, previewed live
  • Copy codes from notifications
  • Automatic scheduled backups
  • Optional edge-to-edge display
